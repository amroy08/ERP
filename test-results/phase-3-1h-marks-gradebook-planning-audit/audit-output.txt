--- 11. Teacher Marks Authorization Strategy ---
   getTeacherMarksScopeOrThrow(authUser, examId, subjectId):
   1. Verify role === 'teacher'
   2. Load teacher profile with subjectTeachers (include section→class) + assignedClasses
   3. Load exam → get exam.classId and exam.schoolId
   4. School scope check: teacher.schoolId === exam.schoolId
   5. Authorization — at least ONE must be true:
       a. SubjectTeacher: st.subjectId===subjectId AND st.section.class.id===exam.classId
       b. Subject fallback: subject.teacherId===teacher.id AND subject.classId===exam.classId
       c. Class assignment: teacher.assignedClasses includes exam.classId
